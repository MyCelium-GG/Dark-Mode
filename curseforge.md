# Dark Mode

**Turn the whole game dark, in one install.** Menus, inventories, books, the creative menu and the red Mojang loading screen all go dark.

Screens from other mods go dark too, so you don't need a separate resource pack for each mod.

![A chest screen in dark mode](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/darkmode/img/chest.png)

## Winter update

- **Christmas loading screen:** a snowy clearing at sunset behind the Mojang logo, with a lit Christmas tree, a log cabin, snowmen, a frozen pond and snowy mountains, built in Minecraft and turned into pixel art
- **Your own loading screen:** drag any PNG onto the Dark Mode settings screen and it becomes your loading screen
- **Winter theme:** icy blue screens with snow falling behind every menu

![The Christmas loading screen](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/darkmode/img/christmas-loading.png)

## What you get

- Dark menus, inventories, chests, furnaces, anvils and books
- Dark screens from other mods, with no per-mod setup
- A black Mojang loading screen, or charcoal, midnight, plum, Christmas or your own image
- Items, hearts and logos keep their colors
- Dark text gets brighter so you can still read it

## Make it yours

Find the **Dark Mode** button in the top left of the Options menu.

- **Themes:** Neutral, Midnight, Forest, Plum, Ember and Winter
- **Brightness:** from nearly black to soft grey
- **HUD:** darken the hotbar too, if you like
- **Mods to leave alone:** keep any mod's screens as they are

![The Midnight theme](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/darkmode/img/midnight.png)

## Good to know

- Client-side: servers don't need it
- Requires [Mycel](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mycel). Without it the game still starts, and Dark Mode tells you in chat that Mycel is missing
- Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, Minecraft 26.1 to 26.3
- On Forge and NeoForge, the very first loading window comes from the loader before any mod runs, so it stays plain. The Christmas scene and your own image show on every loading screen after that.
- If a screen from another mod looks wrong, add that mod under **Mods to leave alone**
- Optional anonymous stats and error reports (FastStats, Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3): nothing is sent unless you agree on the screen at first start. [What is sent](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/privacy#mod-stats)
